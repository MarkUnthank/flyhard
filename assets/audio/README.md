# Recorded car horn

`car-horn-jan-ruttner.wav` is the real recording **05 Horn.wav** by
15HPanska_Ruttner_Jan, published February 28, 2019 under CC0 1.0.

- Original: https://freesound.org/people/15HPanska_Ruttner_Jan/sounds/461679/
- Mirror and license: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Car_Horn.wav
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Exact source and SHA-256: `car-horn-source.json`.

The dynamic horn videos use the recorded attack and loop a crossfaded steady
portion for sustained presses. Sound is cropped and faded to the measured
physical button interval. No beep is inserted from a scenario label or timer.
The recording retains its CC0 dedication rather than the source-code license.
