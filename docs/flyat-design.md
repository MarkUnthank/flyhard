# The Flyat

## Agreed direction

A tiny, very old, beat-up Fiat Panda, based on the green Panda side-profile photograph supplied by Mark. Preserve the reference car's recognisable silhouette and proportions.

## Shape and materials

- Upright, boxy three-door body, flat bonnet, angular windows, and nearly vertical rear hatch.
- Faded green paint, charcoal plastic bumpers and lower side trim, small wheels, and simple roof rails.
- Narrow tyres and plain steel wheels; modest, utilitarian proportions.
- Add age beyond the supplied reference: shallow door dents, chipped paint, small rust patches around wheel arches and sills, sun-bleached plastic, scratched bumpers, and an imperfect touch-up panel.
- Wear should follow plausible exposure and contact points. Keep the vehicle mechanically capable of completing the demo.
- Plain, worn interior: tired fabric seats, scuffed dashboard, simple instruments, and a visibly used steering wheel.

## Fly and cockpit

Adapt the seat and reachable control surfaces to the fly's body while retaining the enclosed Panda cabin and exterior silhouette. Determine the fly-to-car scale during cockpit reach and contact tests.

The fly's legs must physically operate the wheel and pedals. Their measured positions drive the CARLA vehicle. Cinematic cockpit and window views should make that contact readable, and must use poses from the same recorded simulation as the vehicle and neural activity.

Include a visibly worn, reachable indicator stalk and working exterior indicator lamps. The [optional indicator demo](indicators-fallback.md) adds a leg-operated left/neutral/right control. Its reach and leg assignment must be tested rather than assumed from the wheel rig. Provide a camera angle that makes the stalk movement and the matching lamp easy to connect.

## Presentation

Aim for a believable neglected little Italian car. The humour comes from a recognisable fly earnestly operating this battered Panda. Keep distinctive body proportions and weathering consistent across simulation assets, footage, and promotional artwork.

## Asset provenance

The supplied photograph is visual reference only. The repository should contain original or appropriately licensed vehicle geometry and materials, with provenance and attribution recorded before distribution. Do not bundle the supplied stock photograph by default.

## Status

Design direction recorded. Independent wheel and pedal test rigs exist, but the final combined cockpit, indicator stalk, Panda-style vehicle mesh, textures and lamps have not been produced yet.
