# Make the Mini yours

Bought a custom wrap? [Book a meeting with Mark ASAP](https://cal.com/mark-unthank/meeting) so we can get your brief together and make your wrap.

In the meantime, feel free to vibe, sketch, or start designing. Bring your ideas, logos, colours, and references to the call. You don’t need a finished design — we’ll make it together.

- [Download the Mini’s paint texture](./M_Bodywork_Mini2021_d.png?raw=true). This is the 2048 × 2048 bodywork base-colour PNG, extracted unchanged from the packed Blender source. Open it in your preferred image editor and work on a copy. On the GitHub file page, use **Download raw file** to save the PNG.
- [Get the editable Blender model](../the-driving-fly-mini.blend). The bodywork texture is already packed in this file. Use it to see how the texture maps onto the car; some other parts, including the roof and windows, use separate materials. We can handle those together.

These are design starting points for the simulated Mini. Uploading a file here does not apply it to the website or simulator. Bring your artwork to the meeting or reply to your purchase email.
